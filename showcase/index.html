The problem

One announcement. Weeks of work by hand.

A regulator issues an announcement to every institution it supervises. Inside each bank, insurer and capital market firm, a compliance officer starts the same manual road.

Banks, capital market institutions, insurers, finance companies and fintechs all receive the same announcement, and each repeats the same work alone.
